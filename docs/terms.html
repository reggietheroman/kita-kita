1. About Kita-Kita

Kita-Kita is a local-first meeting and attendance application for event organizers. It helps organizers maintain attendee lists, create QR codes, record check-ins, and transfer meeting data between authorized devices.

2. Acceptable use

You agree to use Kita-Kita lawfully and only for events you are authorized to organize or administer.

  • Collect only attendee information that is necessary for the event.
  • Inform attendees about the applicable privacy notice and obtain any required consent.
  • Keep device access and QR transfer codes restricted to authorized personnel.
  • Do not use the app to collect government IDs, health information, or other sensitive data unless legally required and properly covered by your privacy impact assessment.
  • Do not use the app to harass, discriminate against, or unlawfully monitor attendees.

3. Organizer responsibility

The event organizer is responsible for the attendee data entered into the app, including its accuracy, lawful collection, retention, disclosure, access control, and deletion. Organizers must respond to attendee requests to access, correct, export, or delete their data.

4. Local storage and backups

Kita-Kita stores meeting and attendee records on the device. There is no Kita-Kita cloud sync service. You are responsible for securing the device, protecting exported files, and deleting data when it is no longer needed. QR transfers should be performed only between devices you trust.
