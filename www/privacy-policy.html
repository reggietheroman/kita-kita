Privacy Notice — Tapok Attendance App

Effective date: September 1, 2026
Personal Information Controller: the event organizer operating this app. Tapok is local-first software with no cloud sync — the developer does not receive, store, or process attendee data.

1. What personal information we collect

When you use Tapok for event check-ins or participant registration, the app may store:

  • Full name (first name, last name)
  • Contact details (email address, mobile phone number) when provided
  • Attendance identifiers (attendee ID, QR code payload, check-in timestamps)

2. Purpose of collection

Data is processed solely to:

  • Verify attendance and process event check-ins
  • Generate attendance statistics for event organizers
  • Transfer meeting data between authorized devices via QR codes

Lawful basis: Freely given, specific, and informed consent (RA 10173 Section 12(a)) and contractual necessity to administer event participation (Section 12(b)).

3. Data storage and security

  • Local-first: Meeting and attendee data is stored on the device running the app. There is no cloud sync service in Tapok.
  • Encryption: Application state is encrypted before being written to local storage. Encryption keys are held in the platform secure store.
  • No sale: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to advertisers or third parties.
  • Camera: The camera is used only to scan QR codes for check-in and device transfer. Images are not stored.

4. Data retention

Personal information remains on the device until the event organizer deletes individual attendees, deletes a meeting, or uses the wipe-event-data option. Organizers are responsible for deleting data when it is no longer needed.

5. Your rights (RA 10173 Section 16)

Under Philippine law you may:

  • Be informed about how your data is collected and used
  • Access and receive a copy of your data (export via CSV or JSON in the app)
  • Rectify inaccurate information (edit attendee records in the app)
  • Request erasure (delete attendee or meeting data in the app)
  • Object or withdraw consent
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data-subject requests, contact the event organizer operating this app. They hold your data and are the party able to act on access, correction, and erasure requests. For questions about the software itself, email support@reggietheroman.app.

You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
